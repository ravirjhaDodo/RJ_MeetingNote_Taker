# AI meeting notes

After you stop listening (or when the app auto-stops after silence), you can generate meeting notes from your captured transcript.

## In detail vs Summary

- **In detail:** full section-wise notes with tables where helpful.
- **Summary:** shorter version with the same formatting rules.

## Formatting rules

- Written in natural, human tone (not generic AI filler).
- **No emojis** except status indicator balls:
  - Red: blocked or critical
  - Yellow: at risk or needs attention
  - Green: done or on track
  - Blue: pending or not started
- Tables used for action items, decisions, and timelines when they add clarity.

## Export and cloud save

When signed in, generated notes are **auto-saved to cloud** with your meeting. You can also export:

- Markdown (.md)
- Word (.docx-style HTML)
- PDF (print dialog)
- **Copy for Word** (plain Markdown in clipboard)

## Access

- Requires sign-in.
- New accounts receive a **7-day AI meeting notes trial**.
- Admins can extend, pause, or resume this feature per user.
